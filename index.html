Footy President

An Aussie rules club management sim for iPhone. No account, no ads, no coins. Your careers stay on your phone.

Privacy PolicyWhat data the game uses and who sees it (very little). Terms of UseThe rules for using Footy President and in-app purchases.

Support

Found a bug, got an idea, or need help with a purchase? Email oscar.lehuu@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few working days. Telling us your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing helps a lot.

  • Restore Go Pro: open the Go Pro screen and tap Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so they come back on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.
  • Refunds and billing: purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Saves: your careers are stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.
